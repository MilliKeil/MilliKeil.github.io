milli keil

experimenting at the intersection of design, new media and performance

My work lies in the interstice of performing arts, digital media, societal discourse and design, often through participatory and experimental approaches. Since 2022, I have been pursuing the joint masters program Design & Computation between the University of the Arts Berlin and the Technical University Berlin, while also fighting for climate justice together with Klasse Klima. I expanded my artistic practice during a guest semester in the Intermedia Studio at the Academy of Fine Arts and Design Bratislava. Since 2023, I am a scholarship recipient by the Studienstiftung des Deutschen Volkes.
